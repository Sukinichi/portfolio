Welcome to my portfolio! ✨

My name is Janne Nedamo, (aka Sukinichi) and I am a Filipino 2D artist and animator currently based in Finland! I specialize in art for games, particularly in the ✨ cute ✨ art style and ✨ cozy ✨ aesthetics. This website is a collection of my work throughout the years and a sneak peek into my creative process.
